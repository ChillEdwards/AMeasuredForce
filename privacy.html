Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

A Measured Force ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through ameasuredforce.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us. When you submit our contact form, we collect the details you provide, which may include your:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company or organization
  • Any information you include in your message

We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell your personal information.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and communicate with you about your project;
  • Provide the services you request; and
  • Maintain records of our correspondence.

Service Providers

We rely on a small number of trusted third-party services to operate the Site and communicate with you. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

  • Web3Forms — processes and delivers contact-form submissions to us.
  • Google Workspace — hosts our email, where your messages are received and stored.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typography used on the Site; loading fonts may transmit your IP address to Google.
  • Railway — hosts and serves the Site.

Cookies & Local Storage

The Site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. We use a small amount of your browser's local storage for essential, functional purposes only — for example, to remember that you've already seen a one-time on-screen hint. This information stays on your device and is not shared with us or any third party. Because we set no tracking or marketing cookies, no cookie-consent banner is presented.

Data Retention

We keep contact-form correspondence for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and to maintain reasonable business records, after which it may be deleted. You may ask us to delete your information at any time (see below).

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain uses of it. To make a request, email us at info@ameasuredforce.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at info@ameasuredforce.com.
